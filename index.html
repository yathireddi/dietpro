How NutriTrack Works

Designed for simplicity and clinical precision, helping you make informed nutritional choices effortlessly.

01

Configure Profile & Target

Input your age, gender, height, weight, activity level, and goal. NutriTrack computes your BMR, BMI, and calibrated maintenance caloric targets.

02

Log Meals Dynamically

Select from the verified food catalog, enter grams consumed, and see real-time recalculation of calories, protein, carbohydrates, and fats.

03

Balance & Optimize

Receive rule-based recommendations identifying dietary gaps, foods to include or moderate, and sample balanced meal schedules.
